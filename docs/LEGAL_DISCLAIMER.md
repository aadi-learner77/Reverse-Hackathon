# CooL Cryptographic Ledger — Legal & Product Scope Disclaimer

### What CooL Cryptographic Verification PROVES:
1. **Data Integrity & Non-Repudiation**: Proves that the decision payload recorded at decision time has not been modified, tampered with, deleted, or backdated.
2. **Execution Provenance**: Proves that the specific model ID and version recorded executed on the input data commitment at the specified timestamp.
3. **Hardware Enclave Attestation**: (When TEE is enabled) Proves via Phala Network `dstack` quotes that execution occurred in a secure enclave without operator intervention.
4. **Privacy Preservation**: Proves non-repudiation of decision state using salted SHA-256 commitments without exposing raw customer PII.

---

### What CooL Cryptographic Verification DOES NOT PROVE:
1. **Model Correctness**: Does not prove whether the AI model made the mathematically or economically "right" decision.
2. **Fairness & Non-Bias**: Does not prove the absence of racial, gender, or demographic bias in model training datasets or inference weights.
3. **Legal Compliance**: Does not substitute for institutional regulatory compliance, credit policy adherence, or statutory lending authorizations.

---

### Regulatory Statement
The CooL decision ledger guarantees **non-repudiation of record**, ensuring that when regulators or courts request proof of what an AI agent or credit model did, the institution can provide an unforged, cryptographically verifiable receipt that holds up under cross-examination.
